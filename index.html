About the Lab

The Trustworthy and Secure AI Lab is based in the Department of Computer Engineering at Sharif University of Technology. Our research focuses on the security, privacy, and trustworthiness of machine learning systems. We study how these systems can fail or be attacked, how sensitive information can be exposed, and how principled defenses and careful evaluation can improve their reliability in practice.

02 / Research directions

Research Topics

01 / SECURITY

Adversarial Machine Learning

Studying adversarial examples and evaluating methods for improving robustness against malicious inputs.

02 / INTEGRITY

Data Poisoning and Backdoors

Investigating attacks on training data and model pipelines, along with methods for detection and mitigation.

03 / PRIVACY

Privacy in Machine Learning

Understanding information leakage through membership inference and model inversion, and exploring privacy-preserving learning.
